## Multiplying Fractions
Simply multiply both numerators to get a new numerator, then multiply both
denominators to get a new denominator.
**Example**:
$$
-\frac{20}{9} \cdot -\frac{3}{2} = \frac{60}{18} = \frac{30}{9}
$$

### Fractions Within Fractions
If the numerator or denominator of a fraction contains another fraction, you 
must treat the outer fraction as a division problem
$$
\frac{\frac{2}{3}}{\frac{4}{5}} = \frac{2}{3} \div \frac{4}{5}
$$
When dividing 2 fractions, simply invert the second fraction and multiply
straight across
$$
\frac{2}{3} \cdot \frac{5}{4} = \frac{10}{12}
$$

#### Other Examples
$$
\frac{\frac{16}{9} - 4}{\frac{4}{3} - 2} =
\frac{16 - (4 \cdot 9)}{9} \div \frac{4 - (2 \cdot 3)}{3} =
\frac{16 - (4 \cdot 9)}{9} \cdot \frac{3}{4 - (2 \cdot 3)}
$$
