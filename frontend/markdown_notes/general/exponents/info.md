## Relationship
If $5^2 = 25$ then $\sqrt{25} = 5$.

### Fractional Exponents
$x^\frac{1}{2}$ is equivalent to $\sqrt{x}$. Likewise, $x^\frac{1}{3}$ is equal
to $\sqrt[3]{x}$.

### Simplifying Roots
Take the number under the root symbol and simplify. Use the example below.
$$
\sqrt{8} = \sqrt{4 \cdot 2} = \sqrt{4} \cdot \sqrt{2} = 2\sqrt{2}
$$
